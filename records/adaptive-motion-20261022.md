# Adaptive direct-PWM motion, 22 October 2026

The robot's motor output changes with battery voltage. Timed PWM is therefore a poor way to command a distance or angle: the human observed smooth 180° turns at PWM 80 for 5.5 s earlier, while a later 7 s turn at 8.54 V was about 162.5° by LiDAR scan alignment and a still later 7 s observation-only mapping turn at 7.83 V was about 117° by the live SLAM pose. The latter two estimates use different localisation methods, so they demonstrate drift in fixed-time predictions but are not a calibrated voltage curve. Forward scan alignment gave approximately 0.397 m and 0.393 m for two 3 s PWM 50 runs, with roughly 41 encoder units each. Sources: `data/experiments/20261022-121933-14c027/`, `20261022-122713-3ae9dd/`, `20261022-121841-a52fdf/`, and `20261022-122003-14b5dd/`.

The ROS bridge now reads distinct Pi UART samples instead of treating repeated 20 Hz HTTP reads as fresh wheel samples. Each new encoder displacement updates a per-wheel speed estimate. The existing speed-at-PWM profile supplies initial motor power only; as measured speed falls, the bridge raises direct PWM smoothly (up to 80), and lowers it if speed recovers. Direct forward goals still finish by measured displacement, while direct turns accumulate map-frame yaw across the ±π boundary when available. A requested wheel barely moving for 1.5 s at saturated PWM is reported as blocked. A low voltage reading alone does not stop motion; expired authority, telemetry/scan loss, and hardware failure still do.

The Pi mission budget now charges actual conservative wheel travel (the greater absolute side displacement), plus maximum possible movement since the newest UART sample and the profile's fallback stopping envelope. Previously, a 10 m allowance was consumed at the profile's maximum speed even if the robot barely moved. The ten-minute mission deadline remains independent of travel. A reset or implausible encoder jump is rejected rather than credited as movement.

Nav2's progress window is now 15 s, allowing slower but real travel; the saturated-wheel stall check can end a genuinely stuck drive sooner. No autonomous movement profile is installed. This controller and budget change passed replay/unit checks but **have not been tested in a live closed-loop drive**. Next validation should compare commanded and LiDAR-measured distance/heading as the voltage varies, record whether PWM tracks the speed target without surging, and verify stopping and quota outcomes. The saved occupancy map is observation-only and cannot authorise navigation.

The complete local test suite passed 124 tests. Deployment was attempted after the changes, but `ugvpi.local` stopped resolving and the previously reachable Pi did not answer at its earlier address. The changes are therefore **local only** until Pi connectivity returns. The cause of lost connectivity was not established; a battery brownout and a Wi-Fi interruption are both possible.
